Compaction qualification is fail-closed and now models its intentional source deltas:
- 2 assignment statements removed from the self-source;
- 1 numeric literal removed;
- parameter/call/discard/control structure invariants remain checked;
- static projections are never substituted for native measurements.

The prepared parameter phase uses ONE packed i64[64] lane instead of five separate arrays, encoding:
owner_function_id + name_identity + type_id + abi_index + value_id.
This avoids adding at least 320 initializer literals to a source whose native value baseline was already 1213/1460.

The parameter candidate also has a static preflight for values/blocks/functions and a native verifier. Even a native parameter PASS remains candidate-only until the reports are reviewed.

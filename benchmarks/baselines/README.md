# s3bench Baselines

Selected `s3bench 1.0.0` baselines may be committed only after schema and
checksum validation, complete raw execution on a clean recorded SHA, full
environment/toolchain metadata, and human report review.

`../baseline-0.8-e2.json` remains historical and must not be moved, overwritten,
or silently converted. Smoke and shared-CI results are not authoritative.

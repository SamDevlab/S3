## Reopen exclusion

P14 evidence does not reopen a production line merely because the target is
large, hot, repeated-address, or structurally reduced. Reopening requires a
new compiler SHA, workload class, memory provenance, alias or mutation fact,
semantic proof, or an explicit reason the unknown is resolvable.

before selecting any future experiment. P14.3 was not started.

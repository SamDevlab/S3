# S3-ZK-0054 - A possibility ledger does not by itself authorize a rewrite

## Claim

Naming the realizations still available for a value is useful for research,
but a smaller possibility set is not evidence that the discarded form was
safe or valuable to restore.

## Evidence

P8.2 compared a `P(v,p)` possibility-set ledger with direct producer/consumer
mismatch rules. The latter explain the shipped P5-P7 cases with measured
eligibility and conservative fallbacks. P8.1 observer, failure, memory-validity,
ABI, and successor witnesses remain counterexamples to a global materialization
elision rule.

## Consequence

Use the ledger to organize candidates and required witnesses. Promote only a
bounded local transformation with a falsifier, exact safety proof, and focused
measurements. The P8.2 selection is `NO_VALID_TARGET_YET`.

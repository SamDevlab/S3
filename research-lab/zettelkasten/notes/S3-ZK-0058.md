# S3-ZK-0058 - A hot safety family can still have no sound optimization target

## Claim

Material native safety realization does not imply a promotable elision. A
candidate must also preserve the exact object, index, length, validity,
provenance, mutation and failure-order obligations at every invalidator.

## Evidence

P9.1 reproduced the P9 `MODELLED_NATIVE_DYNAMIC_COUNT` total of 289500 across
15 workloads. Explicit safety realization was 65224 modelled dynamic x86 lines
(22.529879101900%); bounds were 17464 (6.032469775475%), and memory
initialization was 6660 (2.300518134715%). The minimum local-constant,
success-edge, loop-alignment and same-object check-reuse models found zero
proven eligible sites. Cold failure setup had zero modelled dynamic weight.

## Consequence

Bounds and validity remain a material research family, but P9.1 closes with
`NO_VALID_TARGET_YET`. Register initialization remains P8 territory. The
next discriminating question is whether a proof-bearing loop-carried access
can preserve object/index/length identity through the Assembly contract while
closing all invalidators. The absence of checks in C/GCC is not a safety proof.

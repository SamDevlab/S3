# S3-ZK-0039 - Reset trace coverage is not semantic deadness coverage

## Claim

Observing every reset byte dynamically does not prove that any reset is safe to
remove. Alias, call, failure and lifetime observers can be absent from a
same-object sequential scan.

## Evidence

JSMN closure traced all 14367 allocated-memory reset bytes, but semantic reset
classification remains unavailable.

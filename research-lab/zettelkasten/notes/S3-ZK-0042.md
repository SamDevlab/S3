## Historical claim

An optimizer verifier failure before execution was not evidence for or against
reset deadness and could not be hidden by a skip or stale expectation.

## Historical evidence

In the measured P5 research state, the valid mutable slice closure passed at O0
while O1 failed with `slice_load result does not match element type` in the
optimizer verifier.

## Current interpretation

This remains valid evidence about that historical compiler state and about the
methodological need to separate an independent verifier failure from the reset-
deadness question.

It is **not** a current S3 limitation. `[[S3-ZK-0045]]` records the later
SSA-to-IR metadata-loss correctness fix.

Do not cite this note to claim that current O1 slice optimization still has the
same failure without fresh reproduction on a declared SHA.

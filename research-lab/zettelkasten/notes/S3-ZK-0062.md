# S3-ZK-0062 - Frame residency and spill are different causal phenomena

P10.1 decomposed the frozen A+B sidecar's broad frame population of `39081`
dynamic events into `14795` logical frame-value lines and `24286` other frame
representation lines. `10063` of the logical value events were observed with
stack-resident registers, but stack residence alone does not prove true spill.

The sidecar did not carry exact live-before/live-after sets, frame slots,
call-clobber survivors, physical capacity pressure or an equivalent
allocation/layout counterfactual. True spill/reload causality therefore
remains unestablished. TMOV frame materialization was only recorded (`240`
dynamic events) and not reopened after Correction B.

Permanent decision:

```text
CAUSAL_CLASSIFICATION_COVERAGE=1.0
AVOIDABILITY_CLASSIFICATION_COVERAGE=1.0
TRUE_SPILL_CAUSALITY_ESTABLISHED=NO
CONCRETE_NON_SPILL_AVOIDABLE_EXAMPLE_FOUND=NO
P10_1_SELECTION=NO_VALID_TARGET_YET
FRAME_LINE_STATUS=CLOSE
```

Frame traffic is a representation observation, not an optimization target,
until a causal boundary and a semantics-preserving counterfactual are shown.

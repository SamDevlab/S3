# S3-ZK-0026 — Demonstration success is not technology maturity

TYPE: PERMANENT
STATUS: SUPPORTED

## Thesis

A compiler research prototype, benchmark win or isolated demo must not be promoted to production maturity without integration, coverage and reproducibility evidence.

## Source basis

Roland/Shiman's history of DARPA Strategic Computing documents ambitious architecture/AI programs, infrastructure, demonstrations and the organizational difficulty of connecting research visions to deployed systems. The book is historical evidence, not a compiler algorithm source.

## S3 inference

Research progression must distinguish:

```text
IDEA
PROTOTYPE
COUNTEREXAMPLE-TESTED MODEL
S3 INTEGRATION EXPERIMENT
PRODUCTION CANDIDATE
MERGED/MATURE CAPABILITY
```

A result cannot skip these states merely because a demo is visually or numerically impressive.

- [[S3-ZK-0007]] exact oracle

## Process consequence

Keep the research branch non-production and port only proven mechanisms to a fresh current-main branch under exact-head gates.

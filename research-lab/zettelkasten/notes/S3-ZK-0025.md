# S3-ZK-0025 — A bounded compiler proof language may preserve useful facts cheaply

TYPE: HYPOTHESIS
STATUS: OPEN

## Thesis

Rather than retaining arbitrary logical formulas, S3 may benefit from a deliberately bounded fact language whose expressiveness is chosen so inference/validation remains predictable.

## Source basis

Logic in Tehran contains work on bounded arithmetic where syntactic restrictions on formulas correspond to computational classes, and on quantifier elimination/explicit definability where richer statements can sometimes be reduced to simpler forms.

## S3 inference

A compiler fact language could intentionally support a small decidable/cheap domain such as:

```text
constant equality/inequality
interval/range facts
nonzero/nonnegative
small congruence facts
value equivalence
representation validity
memory-version validity
```

The key design variable is not maximum expressiveness but the strongest useful fact language with bounded compile-time cost.

## Experiment

Inventory facts independently rediscovered or lost across current S3 phases. Determine whether a tiny normalized fact language can preserve at least two high-value facts across a lowering boundary and remove redundant work without introducing a general theorem prover.

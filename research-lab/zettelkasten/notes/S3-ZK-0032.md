# S3-ZK-0032 - Byte-frame metric is broader than initialization metadata

TYPE=PERMANENT
STATUS=SUPPORTED

## Atomic claim

The byte-frame metric used in the P4/P5 investigation includes both initialization-state accesses and ordinary trit value-byte accesses.

## Evidence

P5-AUDIT reproduced 5638 accesses and separated 4589 register-initialization accesses, 837 memory-initialization accesses, and 212 trit value-byte accesses.

## S3 implication

The aggregate metric must be decomposed before it is used to describe one specific state category.

## Decision

SUPPORTED

# S3-ZK-0059 - Explicit range facts did not yield a safe target

`P9.2` tested whether a bounded Assembly-to-emitter range-fact contract could
remove indexed safety checks. On the correctness candidate A+B, a recompute
model over validated CFG facts covered 15 workloads, but found no
path-complete, failure-order-preserving, object/index/length proof. Every
negative control retained its check.

The historical P9 model reproduced at `289500`; A+B measured `289512`. The
`+12` is a correctness-only structural delta from the two fixes, not a P9
gain. The P9.1 classification remained complete:
`REQUIRED_DYNAMIC=65224`, `AVOIDABLE_DYNAMIC=0`, `UNKNOWN_DYNAMIC=26456`.

Model A was sufficient and Model B was unnecessary. Model C, a public Assembly
contract, was rejected because no concrete fact loss required format expansion.
The first observed boundary remains
`ASSEMBLY_TO_EMITTER_RANGE_FACT_CONTRACT_NOT_EXPLICIT`, which is not by itself
evidence that public proof transport is safe or useful.

Permanent decision: `P9_2_SELECTION=NO_VALID_TARGET_YET` and
`P9_PRODUCTION_STARTED=NO`.

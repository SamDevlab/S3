# S3-ZK-0052 - P8.1 found no valid production target yet

## Claim

The post-P7 residual cost is real, but the P8.1 evidence does not establish a
small sound production transformation.

## Evidence

The 12-workload exact-head profile passed emulator and Linux native execution
for every workload. It measured 10,459 native instructions, 5,093 approximate
load/store/address mnemonics, 2,365 branches and 33 calls in aggregate. The
largest repeated family is consistent with initialization and checked native
materialization, but the obligation witnesses also show valid failure,
instruction-limit, memory-validity and ABI observers. No comparable before /
after candidate measurement exists.

## Consequence

P8 selection remains `NO_VALID_TARGET_YET`. No production branch, compiler
change, PR, full candidate suite or remote CI cycle is justified by this
campaign. The next experiment is a path-complete observer-aware attribution
probe, not a blanket metadata-elimination pass.

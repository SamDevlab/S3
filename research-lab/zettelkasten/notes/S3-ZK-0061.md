# S3-ZK-0061 - A hot dynamic family is not an optimization opportunity

P10 counted the exact native sidecar on the frozen A+B correctness candidate:
289512 modelled dynamic lines across 15 workloads and 30 O0/O1 runs. The
primary census is complete, with instruction-limit accounting at 99036,
bounds at 56500, frame canonicalization at 39081, semantic payload at 38050
and memory validity at 29408.

The largest non-semantic families are not automatically targets. Instruction
limit is policy-required under the current evidence; bounds and memory
validity are closed by P9; semantic payload is real work; and frame traffic is
not proven spill. The frame family remains an UNKNOWN avoidability population,
but no concrete removable site was found and its subcomponents are not
separated into true spill, reload, ABI traffic or metadata staging.

Permanent decision:

```text
CLASSIFICATION_COVERAGE=1.0
PROVABLY_AVOIDABLE_DYNAMIC=0
POTENTIALLY_AVOIDABLE_DYNAMIC=0
CONCRETE_AVOIDABLE_EXAMPLE_FOUND=NO
P10_SELECTION=NO_VALID_TARGET_YET
NEXT_GLOBAL_TARGET=NONE
```

Do not select a target only because its counter is large. A future campaign
needs one bounded causal question and one concrete falsifiable removable
example before production work is authorized.

# S3-ZK-0060 - UNKNOWN is not an optimization opportunity

P9.3 reconciled the `UNKNOWN_DYNAMIC=26456` population left by P9.2 on the
validated A+B correctness candidate. The P9.1 ledger assigns the entire
population to `REGISTER_INITIALIZATION_CHECK`: 30096 static check lines,
8892 structural sites and 26456 dynamic events across six workloads.

This is an existing P8 mechanism, not a P9 bounds/validity class. Range facts
cannot establish register initialization, and no P9-relevant UNKNOWN event
remains after the exact ledger partition. The mutually exclusive causal
partition has complete coverage, with every other requested identity,
aliasing, path, mutation, lifetime, overflow, failure-order and
instruction-limit category equal to zero.

Permanent decision:

```text
UNKNOWN_CLASSIFICATION_COVERAGE=1.0
P9_RELEVANT_UNKNOWN_DYNAMIC=0
MAX_THEORETICAL_AVOIDABLE_DYNAMIC=0
P9_3_SELECTION=NO_P9_BOUNDS_OPPORTUNITY
BOUNDS_VALIDITY_LINE_STATUS=CLOSE
P9_PRODUCTION_STARTED=NO
```

Unknown is therefore a classification state, not evidence of a removable
check. Do not increase range-analysis complexity merely to convert this P8
population into an apparent P9 opportunity.

## Research question

Separate the directly established initialization observations from the still-unclassified remainder across JSMN and focused corpora.

## Method

Temporary research instrumentation collected observer class, site frequency, and initialization-related observations across JSMN and focused call, reference, slice, numeric, and phi-heavy workloads.

## Result

The measured subset is reproducible for the studied corpora. The remaining population is not established by this experiment and stays open.

## Durable interpretation

Use this result as bounded evidence for later target selection. The temporary instrumentation itself is research scaffolding rather than a production capability.
